Re-walk the field. See what emerges.

Drop a V-Sealed coin or certificate and enter the issuer's seed. The verifier rebuilds every dot from the seed, compares it to the file, checks the commitment, the state image and, for a certificate, that every printed result matches the sealed finding. Nothing leaves this page: the computation is WebCrypto in your browser.

§ 01 · Input

The demo files in this kit were sealed with the passphrase SAS-master-demo. A real issuer never publishes its master; it publishes the per-token seed, or runs verification as a service, and the commitment is anchored on the register.
