The mark, as files

One mark, three forms, struck in vector.

The Sovereign AI mark is a struck seal: a medallion for documents and certificates, a glyph for favicons and inline lockups, and a level badge for the products that carry a certificate. Every file here is SVG, scales without limit, and comes in the two finishes the Hallmark permits: silver on graphite, and single-colour graphite on paper.

§ 01 · The medallion

The full mark.

A milled rim, an engraved ring, and the device: the Greek letter Ψ fused with the P&ID compressor wedge. Three prongs for the three levels, a wedge that narrows as control tightens, and the apex star above. Use it on certificates, the register, official documents and anywhere the Standard itself speaks. It is the Standard's device, not a vendor's: a certified product displays a level badge, never the medallion.

§ 03 · Level badges

What a certified product displays.

The device carries the metal and the level: pewter for Level 1, sterling for Level 2, champagne for Level 3. The apex is the only warm strike; everything below it stays cool, which is what makes Level 3 read as rare. Each badge comes in a dark and a light version so it sits on either kind of page without alteration.

§ 04 · Rules of use

The mark means one thing. Keep it meaning that.

Who may use it
Only an organisation holding a current certificate, only for the product named on the certificate, and only at the level held. Use that implies an uncertified product in the same portfolio is covered is a withdrawal condition, not a warning.
Always state the level
The badge carries the level and the level name. Do not crop the badge to the device alone, and do not use the medallion or the bare glyph in place of a badge: those are the Standard's marks, not a certificate holder's.
Colour
Silver on graphite, or single-colour graphite on paper. The dark and light badge files already carry the right values. Never recolour, never add a second accent, and never apply champagne to anything but Level 3.
Clear space & minimum size
Clear space on every side equals the height of the glyph in the badge, roughly a quarter of the badge height. Minimum sizes: badge 180 px wide on screen, 40 mm in print; medallion 48 px or 12 mm; glyph 16 px.
Proportions & effects
Scale uniformly. No stretching, rotation, outlines, drop shadows, gradients of your own, or placement over busy imagery. The files are vector; use them as supplied.
Where it links
On a web page the badge links to the holder's entry on the public register, so a buyer can verify the claim in one click without contacting anyone.
When it stops
On expiry, suspension or withdrawal the badge comes down within ten business days. The register shows the status either way.
§ 05 · Permitted claims

Say exactly what the certificate says.

The wording a marketing or sales team may use alongside the badge. Anything beyond it needs the Standard's written agreement.

Say

  • "[Product] is Sovereign AI Certified at Level 2 (Controlled) against Sovereign AI Standard v1.0."
  • "Independently assessed by [accredited assessor]; certificate [number], valid to [date]."
  • "Verify on the public register at register.sovereignai.com."
  • "Certified across all seven dimensions of AI control: model, data, provenance, operational, infrastructure, exit and evaluation."

Do not say

  • "Sovereign AI approved", "endorsed", "accredited" or "compliant". The certificate evidences criteria; it endorses nothing.
  • "Safe", "accurate", "unbiased" or "fit for purpose". The Standard certifies control, not quality.
  • "EU AI Act compliant" or "regulator recognised". No regulator has recognised the Standard.
  • "[Company] is certified". Certification is product-scoped.

The mark. SovereignAI is a certification mark of Sovereign Technology Systems Corporation, USPTO application Serial No. 99861987, registration pending. The mark is licensed to certificate holders under the Standard's mark usage terms, whichever accredited assessor issued the certificate, and is not owned by any assessor. The certification statement as filed: "Artificial Intelligence programs, algorithms, and tools that are architected to maximize the privacy, self-determination, and sovereignty of the user over their data, knowledge, and cognitive models."

§ 06 · Certificate & seal

What a certificate looks like, and what seals it.

An A4 landscape template on paper: the medallion, the level badge, the fields a certificate must carry, a verification link to the register, and a band at the foot reserved for the V-Seal microprint field. The V-Seal is the Standard's tamper-evident seal: a keyed field of sub-pixel traces across the artwork from which the device itself emerges on verification. Its method is published in the V-Seal guide.
