01 — Why it's invisible

Three things about a book like yours hide the AI underneath it.

Concentration

It began with one company

The wealth often started with a single business — and the position, or the stock you took when you sold it, may now trade on an AI story you never underwrote.

Direct deals

You back companies yourself

No bank sits between you and the risk. The AI claims in the data room are yours to test — and the ones you've already backed were tested against a market that has since moved.

Opacity

Private positions don't resolve

Your illiquid, co-invested and direct holdings never appear cleanly in a factor model. The exposure is real; the measurement simply isn't there.

Addepar shows you what you own and how it has performed. It was never built to price the AI underneath it — and that layer is now where a growing share of the risk lives.
