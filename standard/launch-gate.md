## G06 Appeals — a structural note

Appeals go to the body that writes the criteria. That is defensible, the way a standards body's interpretation panel works, and the assessor who made the determination is not on it, which is the ISO/IEC 17065 requirement. Two things would make it hold up under scrutiny:

- Each appeal panel includes at least one person from outside Sovereign Technology Systems Corporation.
- State plainly on the governance page whether Tensor has any ownership, funding or personnel link to Sovereign Technology Systems Corporation. If it has none, say so. If it has one, the independence claim needs restructuring, not wording.

## G07 Assessor accreditation — proposal

**Granting body.** An Accreditation Committee of Sovereign Technology Systems Corporation, separate from the Lab's Research Group. The body that admits graders should not be the body that hears appeals against graders.

**Criteria for admission**

1. A legal entity with professional indemnity insurance at a stated minimum.
2. A named lead assessor and at least two assessors who have completed criteria training and passed a calibration exercise across all seven dimensions and both tracks.
3. An impartiality policy: no readiness consulting or remediation advice to any organisation the firm may assess, no fees from model, cloud or reseller suppliers, a conflicts register, and a referral path to another assessor when a conflict cannot be managed.
4. A documented method for evidence handling, verification and determination, with a retention schedule.
5. Two witnessed assessments graded within tolerance of a reference panel's grading of the same evidence.
6. Acceptance of surveillance: annual calibration, review of a sample of certification files, and re-accreditation every three years.

**Fees, published.** An application fee, an annual accreditation fee, and a per-certificate register levy. The mark licence is paid by the certified vendor to the Standard, not through the assessor. Suggested starting points: application 7,500; annual 15,000; levy 500 per certificate. These are placeholders to react to, not a costing.

**External accreditation.** Seek ISO/IEC 17065 accreditation for the scheme and its assessors after twelve months of operation or twenty certificates, whichever comes first.

## G08 Complaint route — proposal

1. One address, `complaints@sovereignai.org`, plus a web form on the governance page. Complaints go to the Standard, never to the issuing assessor.
2. Acknowledgement within five business days. Triage by Sovereign AI Lab within fifteen: credible, or not.
3. A credible complaint is put to the certificate holder, who has fifteen business days to respond.
4. Review by the issuing assessor under Lab oversight, or by a different accredited assessor if the complaint concerns the assessor's own work.
5. Outcomes: no action, corrective action with a deadline, suspension, or withdrawal. Each is recorded on the register with its date.
6. The complainant's identity is withheld from the holder unless they consent. Anonymous complaints are accepted and weighted accordingly.
7. Outcomes are appealable through the Research Group. Complaints about an assessor go to the Accreditation Committee.
8. Annual complaint statistics are published.

## G13 Tensor trust page — consolidation

Replace the nine markers with four published policies and drop the "To confirm before publication" box.

1. **Retention schedule.** Raw evidence and working analysis: clean room only, destroyed within ten business days of close. Assessment and evidence index: retained seven years so findings stay auditable, deleted earlier on written request where the engagement letter allows. De-identified scores: retained in the benchmark panel with company-identifying fields removed; opt-out at engagement.
2. **People.** Every person with access is named before access is provisioned and background-screened to a stated level. No subcontracting without written client consent; any subcontractor is bound by the same terms.
3. **Conflicts.** A conflicts check before every engagement. Prior assessment of any company in the client's process within the last 24 months is disclosed. Recusal where a conflict cannot be managed. The written policy sits in the security pack.
4. **Output licence.** The client owns the assessment, findings and evidence index and may share them with counterparties and advisers. Tensor retains its methodology.
